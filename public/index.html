Welcome

Four steps, start to settlement

No app-to-app friction, no waiting on a single processor. Just a request, a match, and a confirmation.

01

Create a request

Set the amount, payment method, and recipient. Attach a reward to make it attractive to fulfillers.

02

Network reviews it

Verified peers browse open requests and accept the ones that match their available payment rail.

03

Funds move directly

The fulfiller sends payment through the chosen method. Both sides confirm the transaction in-app.

04

Reward is released

Once confirmed, the reward settles automatically and the request closes out on both dashboards.
